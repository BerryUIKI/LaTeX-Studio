### 🛠️ Detect TeX Toolchains

LaTeX Studio automatically discovers major TeX distributions installed on your system:
- **TeX Live** (Cross-platform, recommended)
- **MiKTeX** (Windows lightweight distribution with on-the-fly package installation)
- **MacTeX** (macOS dedicated distribution)
- **TinyTeX** (Minimalist, lightweight distribution)

Click below to verify your environment:
[🚀 Check TeX Environment](command:latex-studio.checkEnvironment)
